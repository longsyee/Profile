## Design Context

### Users
Potential employers, collaborators, and clients visiting Edwin's developer portfolio to understand his work and start a conversation.

### Brand Personality
Expressive, thoughtful, technically curious. The experience should feel confident, memorable, and easy to explore.

### Aesthetic Direction
An electric print studio: continuous deep-ink canvas, warm-paper type, cobalt and coral accents, and restrained acid lime. Use bold editorial typography, mono labels, crisp borders, and print/halftone details. Avoid disconnected color worlds and ornamental crystal or aurora imagery.

### Design Principles
- Keep every landing-page section within one visual world; vary composition and accent placement.
- Keep project records, uploads, links, and the private editor behavior authoritative.
- Make the process story understandable and operable without scroll motion.
- Let the complete TV head respond subtly to fine pointers; keep it still on touch and reduced-motion settings.
- Keep type, focus, and calls to action readable at narrow widths.
